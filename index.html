What it does

Eight things, and they all read from each other

Game model

Identity, style of play by moment, formations with diagrams, principles and sub-principles, and the role profile each position owes. Everything else reads from it.

Periodization

Every training day and fixture in the season, with blocks, game day, session type and load. Cancel a day, record who covered it, and see how the week landed.

Session planner

The identified game situation, the instance you are trying to see, its components and filters, the shape both teams take, and activities with diagrams. Prints to one page or to full detail.

Player development

A development sheet per player built on benchmark categories and tiers, a depth chart that works out age and birth-year band from a date of birth, and cycles that aim a block at named players.

KPIs

Define what success looks like, set the target as successes out of opportunities, tally it on your phone at the field, and see which players are actually succeeding.

Video and analysis

Freeze a frame from a clip and draw on it. Classify actions with outcomes and zones. Count a whole game and turn it into a benchmark you can set a target against.

Team sheets and minutes

Starting XI and substitutes against each fixture, minutes worked out rather than typed, and a training week that asks something different of the player who played ninety and the one who played none.

Coach observation

Watching another coach, with a timed intervention log: who, when, how long, and where. It counts the interventions, the stopped play and how many different players got coached.

How it fits together

One chain, from the model to the pitch and back

  1. Game modelWhat you want the team to look like, moment by moment.
  2. PrincipleThe idea you are coaching, with its sub-principles underneath.
  3. StrategyWhat you are going to do about it.
  4. Identified game situationOne sentence: when we do this, that happens, because of this.
  5. InstanceThe observable components that tell you the moment is on the pitch.
  6. SessionActivities built to make that instance appear, with the numbers the game size asks for.
  7. ReviewDid it appear, did the players succeed, and was the gap planning or delivery.

Change a principle and the planner, the periodization grid, the KPIs and the role profiles all follow it. Nothing is typed twice.

Your data

It stays on your device. That is the design, not a limitation.

Player names, dates of birth and video never leave the machine you typed them on. There is no server holding your roster, because there is no server. It is why there is nothing to breach.

Nothing is uploaded

Including clips loaded onto the analysis board. They are read from the device and played locally.

You keep the backup

One file holds everything. Move it between browsers, devices or hosts. No lock-in, no export fee.

Works offline

At the field, on a phone, with no signal. It is not waiting on anything.

Yours to keep

The whole thing is files. If this disappeared tomorrow, your copy would still open.

The trade is that clearing your browser clears your work, so the backup matters. The app says so rather than hiding it.
